# Changelog

Notable changes to OpenFinance, for people who use it at home. Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/es-ES/1.1.0/); versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/lang/es/). The technical detail of each item is in the [roadmap](docs/product/roadmap.md) under its `OF-xxx` ticket.

### Added

- **Household and access** (OF-101): a household with two people who sign in with email and password. Sign-up is done once on the server (`make bootstrap`); there is no public registration.
- **Accounts** (OF-102, [ADR-0013](docs/adr/0013-cuentas-personales-privadas.md)): the joint Revolut account, its savings pockets and the personal Bankinter accounts, with their balance. Each personal account is visible only to its owner.
- **Transactions** (OF-103, OF-106): by month, with search, account filter and «solo sin categoría» ("only uncategorized"). Each row says whether it is a contribution and from whom, a transfer to the savings pocket, a refund, income or an expense. Transfers to savings do not count as spending.
- **Import statements** (OF-104, OF-105, OF-107): Revolut CSV or Excel and Bankinter Excel, in three steps (choose, review, confirm). The review says what is new, what was already there, what is discarded and whether the balance matches the bank's. Importing the same statement twice duplicates nothing.
- **Categories** (OF-201 to OF-203): 13 starting categories that you can rename, recolor or hide. When you correct an expense, OpenFinance learns the merchant and classifies the other uncategorized expenses from that merchant. It never changes a category chosen by hand.
- **Charts** (OF-301 to OF-305): the screens «Este mes» ("This month"), «¿En qué gastamos?» ("What do we spend on?") and «Evolución» ("Evolution"), with what was spent and saved, spending by category against your average, the pace of the month and 12 months of history. `make demo` creates a fictional household to see them without real data.
- **My spending** (OF-108): in «Este mes» and «¿En qué gastamos?», the «Hogar / Mío» ("Household / Mine") selector shows the spending from your personal accounts and what you have contributed to the joint one. Only you see it and it does not change the household totals.
- **Savings** (OF-401 to OF-405): budgets per category with a warning at 85 %, savings goals with an estimated date, detection of recurring payments and price increases, savings ideas with their supporting data and a cut simulator.
- **Maintenance** (OF-606): `openfinance users set-password` changes someone's password from the server and closes their sessions; `openfinance accounts add` adds a savings pocket or an account after sign-up. See [Instalar en casa](docs/deployment/home.md) (Install at home).
- **Export and backups** (OF-501, [ADR-0016](docs/adr/0016-exposicion-y-copias.md)): an **Exportar** (Export) button (JSON or CSV) with what each person sees, and `make backup` / `make restore` to save and recover the whole database.
- **Home installation** (OF-502): Docker Compose to use it on the local network or from outside with HTTPS and your own domain. Guide: [Instalar en casa](docs/deployment/home.md).
- **Mobile** (OF-503): all screens and charts work well on mobile.
- **Daily use** (phase 8, OF-801 to OF-806): adaptive menu (bottom bar on mobile, rail on tablet, sidebar on desktop); the month, view and filters go in the URL and can be shared; «Este mes» forecasts the month-end close and warns about missing recurring payments, and fixed payments no longer trigger «cerca» ("close") in budgets; Movimientos (Transactions) loads 50 at a time with the sum of the filter; accessibility (titles, focus, errors next to the field, contrast checked with axe); dark mode and installable on mobile; simulator with all categories and ideas that can be dismissed.
- **Operations** (phase 9, OF-901 to OF-907): backups are encrypted with `age` and rotate automatically (`OPENFINANCE_BACKUP_RECIPIENT`); images and actions pinned by digest, Renovate, `pip-audit`, `npm audit` and Trivy in CI; nginx compresses and caches static files with security headers, and hardened Compose with rotating logs; `OPENFINANCE_LOG_LEVEL` works, every request carries `X-Request-ID` and `/ready` checks the database; balances are summed in SQL and confirming an import does a single flush; the login attempt limit is atomic and bounded, and Argon2 no longer occupies the threads of the rest of the API; images published and signed with cosign on every release and an optional new-version notice (`OPENFINANCE_UPDATE_CHECK`, off by default). See [Instalar en casa](docs/deployment/home.md).
- **Complete ledger** (phase 10, OF-1001 to OF-1008, [ADR-0018](docs/adr/0018-libro-contable-completo.md) and [ADR-0019](docs/adr/0019-varias-monedas.md)): merchants that can be renamed and merged; tags and long notes; splitting an expense into parts with a category; linked transfers between your own accounts; cash, card, loan and investment accounts (tracking only); reviewed transactions and a balance check against the bank; attached receipts and invoices (inside the encrypted backup); and accounts in other currencies with exchange rates that you set yourselves. Everything can be undone from «Mis cambios» ("My changes") and respects the privacy of personal accounts.
- **Rules and universal import** (phase 11, OF-1101 to OF-1105, [ADR-0020](docs/adr/0020-motor-de-reglas-e-importacion-universal.md)): rules with conditions (description, merchant, amount, account, direction…), actions (category, type, tags, note, merchant) and phases; the first one that sets a field wins and what was done by hand is never overwritten; they can be tested without saving and applied to existing data, with undo. Import OFX, QIF and CAMT.053, and any bank's CSV with a profile created from a sample file. They are recognized by content and go through the same preview as always.
- **Planning** (phase 12, OF-1201 to OF-1206, [ADR-0021](docs/adr/0021-pagos-programados-y-prevision.md) and [ADR-0022](docs/adr/0022-presupuestos-con-limites-o-sobres.md)): scheduled payments (rent, insurance, salaries) with their calendar and status (collected, pending or overdue) without recording anything until the bank charges it; balance forecast per account with the first day in the red; budgets that can be a limit or an envelope that carries over what is left over or missing, and a different amount for one month; templates for manual transactions; «hoy podéis gastar» ("you can spend today") in «Este mes», with its account in view and without inventing figures if data is missing; and goals with contributions, priority and a suggested split. Everything can be undone and respects the privacy of personal accounts.
- **Alerts** (phase 13, OF-1301 to OF-1303, [ADR-0023](docs/adr/0023-avisos-y-notificaciones.md)): deterministic warnings in «Este mes» (duplicate charges, fees, rising bills, overdue payments, forecast negative balance and exceeded budgets) that each person can dismiss; optional Web Push notifications with VAPID keys (`openfinance alerts keys` and `openfinance alerts notify` for cron), with no amounts or merchants in the text; and a weekly summary (`#semana`) that is also notified once a week.
- **Bank connection** (phase 14, OF-1401 to OF-1404, [ADR-0024](docs/adr/0024-conexion-con-el-banco.md)): each person can connect their bank through PSD2 with their own Enable Banking account (read-only), link each bank account to a household account and revoke the consent whenever they want. Transactions enter through the same pipeline as a statement (no duplicates, undoable) with «Sincronizar ahora» ("Sync now") or `openfinance bank sync` in cron. A warning (and optional notification) 14 days before the consent expires and when it expires; renewing does not duplicate the history. What you had already recorded and the bank also brings is reconciled instead of duplicated, and Ajustes (Settings) shows the difference with the bank's balance. Off if the server does not have the application configured. Tested with Revolut; Bankinter, pending.
- **Reports and net worth** (phase 15, OF-1501 to OF-1504, [ADR-0025](docs/adr/0025-informes-y-patrimonio.md)): `#patrimonio` shows assets minus debts of the accounts you can see, today and at the end of each month, with other currencies converted (or flagged if the exchange rate is missing).
- **Extended household** (phase 16, OF-1601 to OF-1603, [ADR-0026](docs/adr/0026-hogar-ampliado.md)): up to 10 people per household with roles (administers, edits, view only). Whoever administers invites with a single-use link (valid for 7 days) in Ajustes; there is no public registration without an invitation. The role decides what can be changed, not what is seen: personal accounts remain visible only to their owner. **Expense splitting** (`#reparto`): what one person pays for several is split equally, by percentage, by amount or by weights (the parts always add up exactly to the amount), with guests without an account, balances that sum to zero, suggestions of who pays whom and payments between people confirmed by whoever receives them. **Trips and events** group expenses and summarize how much it cost, who paid, each person's share and how to settle up using only those expenses; once closed they accept no new expenses. Export v2 includes all the splitting data.
- **License** (OF-504, [ADR-0008](docs/adr/0008-licencia-agpl.md)): AGPL-3.0-or-later, with audited dependencies ([docs/legal/dependencias.md](docs/legal/dependencias.md)) and contributions with DCO ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

- **Correct transactions** (OF-701, [ADR-0017](docs/adr/0017-registro-de-cambios.md)): in Movimientos, click the description to change the type (expense, income, contribution and from whom, savings pocket, between accounts) or add a note. Each correction is kept in its history with who and when, and can be undone from the notice. The amount, date and the bank's description are not touched.
- **Several at once and cash** (OF-705): in «Solo sin categoría» ("Only uncategorized") you can select several expenses and give them the same category at once. «+ Añadir a mano» ("+ Add by hand") records an expense or income that does not come from the bank (for example, in cash); it can be deleted and undone.
- **Rules** (OF-702): the Reglas (Rules) screen shows which merchants are classified automatically, into which category and how many transactions they have; they can be deleted. When categorizing, the notice says how many the rule has classified and lets you undo it all. Bizum payments and transfers to people no longer create rules.
- **Import history** (OF-703): Importar (Import) shows which statements have been imported, by whom and how many transactions each brought. The latest import of each account can be undone; its transactions are voided (not deleted) and re-importing the file recovers them with their corrections.
- **Settings** (OF-704): ⚙️ in the header to change your password, close your sessions on other devices and save the card you use to top up the joint account. If someone has not saved it, Importar warns before confirming.
- **My changes** (OF-706): from Ajustes, your latest corrections with «Deshacer» ("Undo") and «Rehacer» ("Redo"). Undo never overwrites what someone changed afterwards; it respects it and tells you who it was. Each person only sees and undoes their own.

### Fixed

- **Import** (OF-601): a statement with a €0.00 row (for example, a card verification) can now be confirmed. Those rows, and the ones with no amount, appear as discarded with their reason.
- **Classification** (OF-602): taking money out of a savings pocket ("From pocket") no longer counts as income; in Movimientos it reads «De la hucha» ("From the pocket"). Names and patterns are matched by whole word: a transfer from «Mariana» is no longer a contribution from Ana.
- **Transactions** (OF-603): searching for text with «#» no longer leaves the list frozen. If a screen fails, «Algo ha fallado» ("Something went wrong") appears with a button to reload.
- **Import** (OF-604): a «1,234» in the Revolut CSV (one thousand two hundred thirty-four) is no longer read as €1.234. Each bank has its own number format; anything that does not follow it, or can be read in two ways, appears as a discarded row instead of being saved with a wrong amount.
- **Current month** (OF-608): Evolución and Este mes give the same average, because both only count complete months. Mid-month each category is compared with what was spent up to the same day in previous months, so on day 1 it no longer looks like everything is far below average.
- **Screens** (OF-605): if something fails to load, the error is shown with a **Reintentar** (Retry) button. Before, a category's transactions panel stayed on «Cargando…» ("Loading…") and Presupuestos (Budgets) did not recover without reloading the page.

### Security

- Passwords with Argon2id, server-side sessions with an `HttpOnly` cookie and a limit on sign-in attempts. API writes require a header against CSRF.
- Neither the database nor the API is published outside the machine. The IP the API sees cannot be spoofed.
- An uploaded, unconfirmed statement is deleted after 24 h even if nobody imports again: on startup, on sign-in and before each `make backup` (OF-607).
- Statements are read with size limits and without executing formulas. The CSV export is protected against formulas.
- A household's data and each person's personal accounts are isolated, and there are tests that verify it.
